Privacy Policy

Screen Share ("Screen Share | Mirror & Cast")

Effective date: June 30, 2026

Summary

The App enables live screen sharing over the local network. This policy explains what data the App may access or transmit, why, and how we handle it.

Data Collected and Usage

  • Local network IP addresses: used for device discovery and establishing peer connections. Not shared with external parties except as needed for WebRTC signaling.
  • In-memory media streams (video/audio): live streamed to connected viewers; by design the App does not persistently store screen video or audio on device or servers.
  • Signaling metadata: temporary identifiers and anonymous tokens used with Firebase Realtime Database to coordinate WebRTC sessions (room IDs, SDP, ICE candidates). These are ephemeral and used only for live connection setup.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Realtime Database (for signaling): used to exchange ephemeral session data. See Firebase privacy documentation for details.

Microsoft Clarity (for analytics and session replay): used to understand how users interact with the App and to improve performance, usability, and reliability. Microsoft Clarity may collect usage data such as taps, screen views, device information, diagnostic information, and app interaction events. We use this information only for analytics and product improvement.

The App may allow users to view screen-sharing sessions from another device. We do not intentionally send shared remote screen or video content to Microsoft Clarity, and remote screen and video areas are masked from Clarity session recordings where applicable. We do not configure custom Clarity events to send local network IP addresses, share URLs, device names, passwords, messages, financial information, or other sensitive personal content.

Microsoft Clarity is provided by Microsoft Corporation. For more information, see Microsoft's Privacy Statement .

Permissions

  • Microphone/Camera: requested only if you explicitly grant permission to include audio/video in a session.
  • Local network access: requested to discover and connect to other devices on your local network.

ReplayKit Extension and App Group

The App uses a ReplayKit broadcast extension to capture screen frames when you start a broadcast. The app and extension share a local App Group container for small control files (e.g., room ID, stop flag) used to coordinate starting and stopping broadcasts.

Data Retention and Security

The App does not persistently store screen video or audio on device or servers. Signaling data used to establish connections is transient and can be removed by the extension after a session ends. Analytics and session replay data collected through Microsoft Clarity is handled by Microsoft according to its privacy terms and is used by us only for analytics, reliability, and product improvement.
